# Scoop marketing kit

Published assets live in `public/media/` so the README, product page and portfolios share the same files.

- `screenshots/`: real app captures at 390 × 844 CSS pixels, 2× resolution, plus a desktop overview. Example labels are preserved.
- `videos/`: two 1080 × 1920 portrait MP4s, 20 seconds each, H.264 video and AAC stereo audio. Original instrumental soundtrack; no narration. A persistent on-video disclosure identifies the sample data.
- `banner.png` and `social-preview.png`: product header and social card.
- `press-kit/`: feature descriptions, suggested tags and ready-to-edit social copy.

Only portrait videos with audio belong in this kit. Old landscape/silent renders are archived under the ignored local-only folder. Local source captures and render projects are not published.
