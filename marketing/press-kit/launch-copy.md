## LinkedIn / longer post
My Fitbit collects the measurements. I wanted a better way to connect them to everyday decisions.

Scoop brings sleep, recovery, strain and stress into one dashboard, with trends, workout details and coaching for today and tonight.

The example dashboard is available without an account. Connecting your own Fitbit uses read-only Google Health permissions and is subject to the OAuth app’s access configuration. Scores are personal estimates, not medical measurements.

Explore: https://scoop-mauve-two.vercel.app/about.html

## Claim boundaries
Do not promise direct tracker sync, clinical accuracy, official Fitbit/WHOOP scores, universal OAuth access. The server does not persist health records, but the browser caches days and stores local settings/check-ins; avoid “stores nothing”. No posts have been published by this task.
