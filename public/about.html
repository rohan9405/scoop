A few useful answers.

Does Scoop replace the Fitbit app?

No. Your Fitbit still syncs through the official app to Google Health. Scoop reads the uploaded cloud data; it does not connect to the tracker over Bluetooth.

Are Recovery and Strain official Fitbit scores?

No. Scoop calculates its own estimates from the measurements available to your device and permissions. Recovery needs at least three days of HRV history. Missing measurements stay unavailable.

Where does my data live?

The server does not persist your health records. An encrypted session cookie holds the connection tokens; cached days, journal entries and settings stay in your browser. Disconnect revokes the token and clears the session.

Is Scoop a medical tool?

No. Recovery, Strain, Stress and Scoop Age are informational estimates. Scoop is an independent project and is not affiliated with Fitbit, Google or WHOOP.
